## v1.22 实施计划

### 1. 新建 `noncommutative_geometry.py` - Noncommutative geometry
- `NoncommutativeSpace` - 非交换空间 (Connes spectral triple)
- `SpectralTriple` - 谱三元组 (A, H, D)
- `DiracOperator` - Dirac算子
- `FredholmIndex` - Fredholm指标
- ` HochschildCohomology` - Hochschild同调
- `CyclicCohomology` - 循环同调
- `KHomology` - K-同调
- `PseudodifferentialOperator` - 伪微分算子
- `ConnesChernCharacter` - Connes-Chern字符

### 2. 新建 `ergodic_theory.py` - Ergodic theory and dynamical systems
- `ErgodicTransformation` - 遍历变换
- `MeasurePreservingMap` - 保测映射
- `ErgodicTheorem` - 遍历定理
- `MixingTransformation` - 混合变换
- `KolmogorovSinaiEntropy` - Kolmogorov-Sinai熵
- `BernoulliShift` - Bernoulli移位
- `PoincareRecurrence` - Poincaré回归
- `InvariantMeasure` - 不变测度
- `ErgodicDecomposition` - 遍历分解

### 3. 新建 `higher_category_theory.py` - Higher category theory
- `InfinityCategory` - 无穷范畴
- `KanComplex` - Kan复形
- `NCategory` - n-范畴
- `WeakEquivalence` - 弱等价
- `HomotopyPushout` - 同伦推出
- `HomotopyPullback` - 同伦拉回
- `SegalCategory` - Segal范畴
- `CompleteSegalSpace` - 完全Segal空间
- `Anima` - Anima (无穷群胚)
- `InfinityTopos` - 无穷拓扑斯

### 4. 新建 `k_theory.py` - K-theory
- `K0Group` - K₀群 (Grothendieck构造)
- `K1Group` - K₁群 (矩阵GL_n的极限)
- `K2Group` - K₂群 (Steinberg群)
- `KRing` - K-环 (λ-环结构)
- `TopologicalKTheory` - 拓扑K理论
- `AlgebraicKTheory` - 代数K理论
- `QuillenK` - Quillen K-理论
- `NilK` - Nil K-理论
- `HigherKGroup` - 高阶K群
- `AtiyahHirzebruch` - Atiyah-Hirzebruch谱序列
