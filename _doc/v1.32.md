## 背景与目标

模仿对象: [mathlib4](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4)

mathlib4 核心覆盖领域（截至 v4.29.1）:
- **Commutative Algebra**: Localization, primary decomposition, Noetherian rings
- **Homological Algebra Advanced**: Spectral sequences, derived functors, Ext/Tor
- **Algebraic Topology Advanced**: Fundamental groupoid, covering spaces, homotopy groups
- **Differential Geometry Advanced**: Connections, curvature, geodesics

lean4py 当前状态：
- v1.31 完成 Algebraic geometry advanced, Automorphic forms, Galois representations, Arithmetic geometry
- 已有交换代数基础（ring_theory.py, algebra.py）
- 缺少局部化、谱序列、覆盖空间、联络等高级概念

## v1.32 实施计划

### 1. 新建 `commutative_algebra_advanced.py` - Advanced commutative algebra
模仿 mathlib4 `Mathlib.RingTheory.Commutative`

- `Localization` - 局部化 S⁻¹R
- `PrimaryDecomposition` - 准素分解（简化）
- `NoetherianRing` - Noether 环
- `IntegralClosure` - 整闭包
- `DedekindDomain` - Dedekind 整环

### 2. 新建 `homological_algebra_advanced.py` - Advanced homological algebra
模仿 mathlib4 `Mathlib.Algebra.Homology`

- `SpectralSequence` - 谱序列（简化）
- `DerivedFunctorAdvanced` - 导出函子 Rf, Lf
- `ExtTorAdvanced` - Extⁿ, Torₙ 计算
- `Hypercohomology` - 超上同调（简化）

### 3. 新建 `algebraic_topology_advanced.py` - Advanced algebraic topology
模仿 mathlib4 `Mathlib.Topology.Category.Top`

- `FundamentalGroupoid` - 基本广群 Π₁(X)
- `CoveringSpace` - 覆盖空间
- `HomotopyGroup` - 同伦群 πₙ(X, x₀)
- `CellComplex` - 胞腔复形
- `EilenbergMacLane` - Eilenberg-MacLane 空间 K(G, n)

### 4. 新建 `differential_geometry_advanced.py` - Advanced differential geometry
模仿 mathlib4 `Mathlib.Geometry.Differential`

- `Connection` - 联络 ∇
- `Curvature` - 曲率张量 R
- `GeodesicAdvanced` - 测地线（指数映射）
- `Holonomy` - 和乐群
- `CharacteristicClass` - 示性类（简化）
